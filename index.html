About

I am a Research Scientist in Alignment and Agents at Skywork AI, led by Prof Shuicheng Yan, working towards accelerating the realization of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). I received my Ph.D. from Xidian University in 2021, supervised by Prof Bo Chen and Prof Mingyuan Zhou, and was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University until 2024, supervised by Prof Bo An.

My research focuses on generative models (diffusion models and large language models), reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), and their applications to AIGC, including multi-step reasoning, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and alignment in multimodal generation. I have also worked on Bayesian models, graph neural networks, self-supervised learning and out-of-distribution detection.

I am looking for research interns interested in LLMs for math reasoning and code generation. Please send me your CV if you are interested.
